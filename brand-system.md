# Sukaleyan — Brand System

*Created 2026-06-23*

---

## Purpose

Personal presence building toward Wine Road credibility (April 2027). The face behind Runic Grapes — art historian, feltmaker, vineyard owner.

---

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Status | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Vineyard life | Active | Visits, seasons, hands-on moments |
| Spot-the-vine finds | Active | Wine imagery discovered in the world |
| Feltmaking | Secondary | When it happens — not forced. Aspirational core, not current output. |

---

## Voice

**Witty, natural, unperformed.**

- Short captions, not essays
- Observations, not explanations
- Dry humor welcome
- First-person, present tense
- No "grateful" energy, no performance

**What it's not:**
- Curated aesthetic Instagram
- Educational/explainer tone
- Over-polished or precious

---

## Rhythm

**1x per week**

Sustainable minimum. More if something happens, but no pressure to manufacture.

---

## Workflow

**Spontaneity-based:**

1. Capture in the moment (phone ready)
2. Post within 48 hours
3. Don't let content sit and go stale
4. No batching, no content calendar

---

## Format

| Format | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| Carousel | Default — multiple angles, context, reveals |
| Single photo | When the image is strong enough alone |
| Reel | When something moves — vineyard moments, process clips. Not forced. |

---

## Platforms

- **Instagram (@sukaleyan)** — primary
- **sukaleyan.com** — portfolio/archive, updated less frequently

---

## Relationship to Runic Grapes

Sukaleyan is the maker. Runic Grapes is the curator.

- Some content overlaps (vineyard, finds)
- Sukaleyan is warmer, more personal
- Runic Grapes is more polished, brand-forward
